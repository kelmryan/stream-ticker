You're set up. To show the ticker:

  1. Go to Extensions → My Extensions, click Activate on Stream Ticker and choose Set as Overlay 1.
  2. Go live (or watch your channel). The ticker runs along the bottom of the video, cycling through Latest Followers, Latest Subscribers and Top Cheers. Empty lists show "Be the first!".

Colors, sizes, scroll speed and Extra Life are optional and set on your dashboard. Changes show up for viewers within a minute.

  3. Come back to this page and refresh it. It should say "Connected as" your name.
